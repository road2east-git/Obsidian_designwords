Trending & Modern Graphic Design

- **Brutalism / Neu Brutalism:** Raw, functional, unrefined, and bold.
- **Y2K Graphic Design:** Early 2000s, chrome textures, pixel fonts, metallic.
- **Cybercore / Cyberpunk:** High-tech, neon, glitch effects, dystopian.
- **Gradient Leak / Spectral Gradients:** Fluid, iridescent, 3D gradients, spectral, blurry.
- **Bento Grid / Bento Layout:** Organized, modular, rounded blocks, clean.
- **Glassmorphism / Glass Blur:** Frosted glass effect, translucent, 3D, layered.

Retro & Nostalgic Styles

- **Retro Futurism:** 80s/90s futuristic, VHS textures, neon.
- **Groovy / Indie Groovy:** 70s inspired, trippy, liquid type, bold pastel.
- **90s Editorial Design:** Grungy, collage-style, photo layering.
- **Vaporwave:** Surreal, 80s/90s nostalgia, pastels, classical statues.
- **Synthwave:** Neon grid, cyberpunk, 80s retro, chrome.
- **Punk / Grunge poster:** Distressed, torn paper, high contrast.

Minimalist & Clean

- **Minimalism / Bold Minimalism:** Simple, functional, ample white space.
- **Scandinavian / Scandi:** Clean, functional, minimalist, natural.
- **Japandi:** Japanese minimalism + Scandinavian functional.
- **Warm Minimalism:** Cozy, soft, earthy, functional.

Artistic, Organic & Experimental

- **Surrealism / 3D Surrealism:** Dreamlike, melting forms, digital collage.
- **Handdrawn Doodles / Scribbles:** Sketchy, spontaneous, artistic.
- **Mixed Media / Collage:** Ripped paper, photo montage, textures.
- **Wabi Sabi:** Imperfect, natural, earthy.
- **Art Nouveau / Botanical:** Fluid, floral motifs, organic.

Maximalist & Ornate

- **Maximalism:** Bold, clashing patterns, more-is-more.
- **Baroque:** Opulent, dramatic, gold accents.
- **Memphis Style:** 80s geometric, bright colors, quirky.
- **Kitsch:** Irony, campy, over-the-top, bright.

Interior Design Styles

- **Organic Modern:** Natural textures, clean, calming.
- **Modern Farmhouse / Cottagecore:** Rustic, cozy, nostalgic.
- **Bohemian / Boho:** Eclectic, earthy, layered, patterns.
- **Mid-Century Modern:** 50s-60s design, geometric, retro.

Useful Design Keyword Modifiers

- **Texture:** Grainy, Film Look, Metallic, Ripped Paper, Washi Tape.
- **Typography:** Liquid Type, Serif Designs, Metallic Typography, Hand Lettering, Bold Display.
- **Color:** Pastels, Muted Palette, Duotone, Vibrant, Neon.
- **Elements:** Bento Grid, 3D Abstract, Visible Grid Lines, Stickers.
