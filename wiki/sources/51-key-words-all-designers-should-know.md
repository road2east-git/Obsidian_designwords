# 51 Key words all designers should know

Adobe Express에서 제공하는 디자이너를 위한 51가지 핵심 키워드 정리 문서입니다. 디자인, 타이포그래피, 색상, 브랜딩 등 4가지 범주로 나누어 설명합니다.

## 주요 내용
- **Design (디자인)**: Alignment, Asymmetry, Bleed, Contrast, Crop, Hierarchy, Illustration, Orphans, Raster/Vector, Resolution, Scale, Symmetry, Trim, White Space, Widows.
- **Typography (타이포그래피)**: Ascender, Baseline, Descender, Font, Kerning, Leading, Lorem ipsum, Serif/Sans serif, Script, Tracking 등.
- **Color (색상)**: Analogous, Complementary, Gradient, Grayscale, Hue, Monochrome, Ombre, Opacity, Palette, Saturation, Shade, Tint, Tone, Triadic.
- **Branding (브랜딩)**: Abstract mark, Branding, Emblem, Lettermark, Mascot, Pictorial mark, Wordmark.
